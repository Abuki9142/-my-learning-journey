# 02. AI Foundations, History & Terminologies

## Big Idea

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is not magic, and it is not just a chatbot. It is a field of computer science focused on building systems that can recognize patterns, make predictions, generate content, solve problems, and support human decision-making.

## 1. Understanding Intelligence

Intelligence is the ability to learn, reason, solve problems, understand information, and adapt to different situations.

In AI, computers use algorithms and data to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

## 2. What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is the development of computer systems that perform tasks such as understanding language, recognizing images, making recommendations, and solving complex problems.

Examples:
- Chatbots that answer questions.
- Recommendation systems used by Netflix.
- Voice assistants that understand spoken commands.
- AI tools that generate text, images, and code.

## 3. Important AI Terminologies

### Artificial Intelligence (AI)
The broad field of creating machines and software that perform intelligent tasks.

### Machine Learning (ML)
A branch of AI that enables systems to learn patterns from data and improve their performance.

### Deep Learning
A type of machine learning that uses multi-layered neural networks to learn complex patterns.

### Neural Network
A computing model inspired by some aspects of how biological brains process information.

### Dataset
A collection of information used to train, test, or evaluate an AI system.

### Model
A system trained on data to identify patterns, make predictions, or generate outputs.

### Training
The process of adjusting a model using data so that it can perform a particular task.

### Inference
The process of using a trained model to produce a prediction or response.

### Generative AI
AI that creates new content, including text, images, audio, video, or code.

### Large Language Model (LLM)
An AI model trained on large amounts of text to process and generate human language.

### Prompt
An instruction or input given to an AI system.

### Natural Language Processing (NLP)
A field of AI concerned with understanding and generating human language.

### Computer Vision
A field of AI that enables computers to analyze images and videos.

## 4. A Brief History of AI

- 1950: Alan Turing proposed an influential test for considering machine intelligence.
- 1956: The Dartmouth workshop helped establish AI as a research field.
- 1980s: Expert systems became an important AI approach.
- 1997: IBM Deep Blue defeated world chess champion Garry Kasparov.
- 2012: Deep learning achieved major success in image recognition.
- 2017: The Transformer architecture was introduced in a research paper.
- 2020s: Generative AI tools became widely accessible for writing, coding, image generation, and other tasks.

## 5. Types of AI by Capability

- Narrow AI: Designed for specific tasks, such as recommendations or speech recognition.
- Artificial General Intelligence (AGI): A proposed form of AI that could perform a broad range of intellectual tasks at a human-comparable level. It has not been established as an achieved capability.

## 6. Why AI Matters in Software Development

AI can help developers:
- Generate and explain code.
- Find possible bugs.
- Analyze data.
- Improve search and recommendations.
- Automate repetitive tasks.
- Build applications that respond to user needs.

AI outputs can be incorrect, biased, or incomplete. Developers must test results, protect data, and apply human judgment.

## 7. Practical Example: Netflix

Netflix uses recommendation technology to help users discover movies and shows based on patterns in viewing activity and other signals.

A recommendation system can be described as:

User activity + Relevant data + Recommendation model = Suggested content

This is a simplified explanation, not a description of Netflix's complete production system.

## Key Takeaways

- AI is a broad field, while machine learning and deep learning are related subfields.
- Data and models play important roles in many AI systems.
- Generative AI can produce new content.
- AI has developed over decades of research.
- AI tools can support software development, but their outputs need verification.
